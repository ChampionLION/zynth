# Zynth Chronicles Lore Bible

Version 1.0, 15 October 2026. Authored narrative specification, not an implemented chapter engine. Approved expansion terminology is adopted below; new regional plots remain provisional until released with versioned content definitions.

## 1 Core premise

A world awakens alongside its Wayfarers. Meaningful growth restores opportunities, pathways and shared understanding. No conquest, compulsory ending or separate story EXP is required.

## 2 Historical background

The world once connected places through practiced crafts, exchanged ideas and maintained pathways. During the Great Stillness these connections became dormant. This is a fictional frame, never a diagnosis of a user's real life.

## 3 The Great Stillness

The world became quiet, not destroyed. Histories faded from use; their potential survived. Avoid villains or disasters that require changing the approved premise.

## 4 Wayfarers

Wayfarer is a narrative identity shared by participants, not a new gameplay class. Existing profession, skills and title remain the player's actual progression identity.

## 5 Restoration

Restoration follows verified quests, missions, achievements, discoveries and chapter conditions. Earned terrain, discoveries and cosmetics do not disappear during rest or absence.

## 6 World constraints

There is no fixed final territory. New regions must explain their connections to established places without rewriting past outcomes. Avoid implying that unseen content has already been earned or completed.

## 7 Tone

Quiet wonder, practical hope and specific recognition. Avoid shame, coercive urgency, exaggerated psychological claims or implying that rest damages the world.

## 8 Canonical terminology

- Zynth Chronicles: overarching fictional experience.
- Great Stillness: the world's dormancy.
- Wayfarers: participants in its awakening.
- Atlas of Remembrance: fictional name associated with the existing Mark Map.
- Chronicle Archive: the personal narrative record.
- Awakening: a verified regional or chapter transformation.

Keep familiar application labels and routes.

## 9 Quests

Quests are real, in-app growth activities. Narrative can give them atmosphere but cannot change their success criteria, type, EXP or evaluation.

## 10 Missions

A mission is a playable linked challenge after seven qualifying quests. It is not an automatic landmark reward. A completed mission may influence a chapter but does not automatically end one.

## 11 Chapters

Chapters have explicit, deterministic narrative conditions and variable lengths. Main, side, regional and profession-influenced arcs are possible. Fourteen-mark map segments are not story chapters.

## 12 Professions

Reference only the actual saved profession and recorded evolution history. Knowledge, creativity, technical craft, social understanding and strategy can influence encounters without assigning a competing class.

## 13 Titles

Use earned application titles verbatim where appropriate. Narrative praise cannot grant a title, promote a profession or imply an unearned rank.

## 14 Skills

Recognize demonstrated, stored skills and discovered hidden skills. Do not reveal undiscovered skills or infer sensitive traits from a story choice.

## 15 Achievements

Engine 11 remains authoritative. Stories may acknowledge a verified achievement; they cannot invent one or grant its numerical rewards.

## 16 Regional structure

Each released region specifies stable ID/version, connections, landmarks, context, deterministic discovery/awakening requirements, authored encounters and textual transformation summaries. Existing map names Foothills, Glasswood, Crossing and Zenith Ridge are visual references, not a claim that separate region progression already exists. Detailed regional plots are provisional.

## 17 Choices

Choices must be explicit and understandable, persist by stable identifiers and avoid irreversible traps. They may affect later text, encounters or cosmetic presentation. They never directly change EXP or authorize ownership.

## 18 Continuity

Committed events and saved choices are canon for that player. Missing history produces a gentle generic entry, not an invented accomplishment. Replays do not duplicate archive entries or rewards.

## 19 Expansion

Release a small coherent arc before increasing the catalog. Version definitions and keep old source labels available for archived records. Resolve contradictions before releasing a new package. Optional AI is constrained to canonical facts and deterministic fallback text.

## 20 Forbidden contradictions

Do not destroy earned progress on absence, call a map segment a completed chapter, treat Mission 7 as an ending, add a second leveling system, invent profession changes, expose private response text, or make narrative generation necessary for quest completion. The world continues to grow; it is not a world the user must finish.
